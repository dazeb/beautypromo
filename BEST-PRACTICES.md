# Tips to Improve Affiliate Performance

- **Create educational content:** skincare routines, tutorials, product comparisons and honest reviews help shoppers make decisions.
- **Promote products with clear use cases:** routine-led recommendations usually convert better than generic product lists.
- **Use one clear call to action:** make it obvious where the reader should click next.
- **Disclose affiliate links clearly:** under the CAP Code, marketing communications should be obviously identifiable as marketing. ASA guidance also explains that affiliate content may need to be labelled clearly when it includes affiliate links or earns commission.[1][6]
- **Avoid unsupported beauty claims:** do not claim products treat, cure or guarantee results unless you have appropriate evidence and the claim is legally compliant.
- **Track what converts:** use dashboard data to identify which products, topics and platforms generate sales.
- **Build evergreen content:** articles, guides and product comparisons can keep sending traffic long after social posts expire.
